# T008: stats tokens for every role

Phase 2 · Model: haiku: one aggregation tweak with a small test · Depends-on: T006 · Covers: R32 (stats)

## Brief
`slowhunch stats` reports tokens per role and must include `collide`. In `internal/report/stats.go`
`Compute`, pre-fill `TokensByRole` with zero `Usage` for each of `gate extract place collide` (use
`provider.Roles` only if that adds no import cycle; otherwise a local list of the four names), then add the
cost of every saved report as v1 does (unknown role keys from old reports are still summed under their own
name). The text output of `stats` (`internal/cli/cmd_stats.go`) then shows a `tokens collide` row.

Test `TestStatsCollide` in `internal/report/stats_test.go`: with no reports the map has the four roles at
zero; with two reports carrying `collide` costs `{in 10, out 2}` and `{in 5, out 1}` the collide entry is
`{15, 3}` and the others are unchanged. Adapt v1 stats tests that compared the exact map.

## Verification
`.harness/verify/v2/T008.sh` exits 0. It runs `TestStatsCollide` and the report and cli packages, and checks
that `stats --json` on a fresh vault contains a `collide` key.
